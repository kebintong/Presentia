Presentia 1.2.1 UPDATE AVAILABLE
Version 1.5.5 is ready — 9 updates since your version. One install brings you up to date.

Presentia now tells you when an update is available.

  • It checks automatically and shows a notice in the corner — never during monitoring.
  • Update notes are easier to read.
  • Online registration is now at the top of the Student Registration page.

Monitoring keeps going while other windows cover the meeting.

  • Meeting minimised? Use Keep monitoring on the Meeting Monitor page.
  • Meetings in Chrome, Edge or Brave: leave a small part showing when you cover them.
  • When monitoring pauses, the status says why.
  • A student can join several classes with the same face, but never twice in one class.
  • New Data tab in Settings, with Delete all data.
  • The same face can't be registered as two students.
  • Fixed "could not reach the registration website" on some laptops.
  • New diagnostic mode for reporting problems.
  • Smooth animations, with a switch to turn them off.
  • Double-clicking the top bar no longer resizes the window by mistake.
