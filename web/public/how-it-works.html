How it works

Presentia takes attendance in online classes by recognising your face in the meeting. You register once, and it takes about a minute.

  1. 1

    Enter your join code

    Your teacher shares a six-character code, like K7M-Q2P. It’s on their Presentia screen, or in your class group chat. Each class has its own code.

  2. 2

    Add your student ID and name

    Use your student ID number and your full name as they appear on your school records, then agree to the privacy notice.

  3. 3

    Do the 20-second face check

    Your camera opens and asks you to look straight ahead, blink twice and turn your head. This shows it’s really you, live, and not a photo. The check runs on your own phone or computer; only three photos are sent.

  4. 4

    Submit, and your teacher accepts you

    Your registration waits until your teacher’s Presentia app collects it. When they accept you, the photos are deleted from this website. Anything not collected is deleted after 14 days.

  5. 5

    Join your classes as usual

    In Google Meet, Zoom or Microsoft Teams, keep your camera on and your face visible. Your teacher’s Presentia recognises you and marks you present — nothing else to do.

Questions

I don’t have a join code.

Ask your teacher. They see it in Presentia when online registration is turned on for your class. You can also ask your teacher to register you in person instead.

The camera won’t start.

Allow camera access when your browser asks. If you said no before, allow it for this site in your browser’s settings and tap “Try again”. Close other apps that use the camera, and use Chrome, Safari or Edge.

The face check keeps failing.

Face a window or a lamp so your face is well lit, take off caps, sunglasses and face masks, and hold the camera at eye level about an arm’s length away. Follow each prompt slowly.

I typed my details wrong.

Register again with the same student ID, on the same phone or browser, before your teacher accepts it. The new registration replaces the old one. After your teacher has accepted you, ask them to fix it.

It says my student ID is already waiting.

Someone already registered that student ID for this class from another phone or browser. If that wasn’t you, tell your teacher — they decide which registration to accept.

I’m in more than one class.

Register in each class with that class’s join code, using the same student ID. Your teacher’s app knows it’s you and adds you to the new class.

Can I use a laptop instead of a phone?

Yes. A laptop webcam works the same way, in Chrome, Edge or Safari.

What happens to my photos?

They wait on this website only until your teacher’s app collects them, then they’re deleted here. Your teacher’s computer keeps a face template so it can recognise you in class. The privacy notice has the details.

For teachers
