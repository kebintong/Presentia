Presentia Privacy notice

Privacy notice

How Presentia uses your information when you register for a class.

What we collect

  • Your student ID number and full name.
  • Three photos of your face, taken during the face check.
  • The class join code, and when you agreed to this notice.

The face check itself (blinking and turning your head) runs on your own device. Only the three photos are uploaded.

Why

So your teacher's Presentia app can recognise you during class and record your attendance. Nothing is used for any other purpose, and nothing is sold or shared.

Where it goes and how long it stays

  • Your registration waits on this website until your teacher's Presentia app collects it.
  • The app then builds a face template on your teacher's computer and deletes your registration, photos included, from this website.
  • Anything not collected within 14 days is deleted automatically.
  • On your teacher's computer, your face template and attendance stay with the class. Your teacher can remove you at any time, which deletes your face template unless you are in another of their classes.

Your choices

Registering is your choice. If you prefer, ask your teacher to register you in person instead. To change wrong details before your teacher accepts them, register again with the same student ID — the new submission replaces the old one. To have your data removed, ask your teacher.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information. Contact your teacher or your school's data protection officer.

Back to registration

Photos are deleted after your teacher's app collects them, or after 14 days.
